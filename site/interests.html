Interests

Research

I’m interested in making learned systems more efficient and the tools we use to study them more trustworthy. That brings together efficient ML, training dynamics, and research systems, with a particular interest in whether interpretability methods and evaluations measure what we think they do.

My published work audits how the fitting corpus changes a Jacobian lens’s readouts. More broadly, I’m exploring model compression and distillation, how capabilities develop during training, and infrastructure that makes interpretability and evaluation cheaper and reproducible.

The common questions are what a measurement actually establishes, what assumptions it depends on, and what it costs to use. These are interests and directions, rather than a list of completed projects.
